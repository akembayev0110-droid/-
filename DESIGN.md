# Quiet Harbor — tactical tabletop
A warm paper surface, ink-black typography and an acid-lime fleet on a deep maritime teal board. Home is an invitation to a tangible tabletop game; setup and battle are calm operating surfaces. References guide the spatial play of Bruno Simon and Choo Choo, directness of Neal.fun, interaction clarity of Linear and typographic confidence of Lando Norris. No borrowed assets.
## System
Manrope supports all three languages. Large two-line headline, asymmetrical hero with a CSS-perspective miniature of the actual fleet, flat opponent selection separated by rules. 12px control radii, 44px minimum ordinary controls, visible focus. Game grids use compact cells with coordinate labels. Motion is limited to board arrival and control feedback, disabled for reduced motion.
