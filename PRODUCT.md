# Тихая гавань
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Product Purpose
Complete classic Battleship for a student assignment. Guest players play a bot, invite a friend or use matchmaking.
## Capabilities and Constraints
RU, KK and EN. Three bot difficulties. Classic fleet and no touching. 60 second turns and a separate 60 second reconnect grace. Saved local layouts and games, surrender, rematch, wins and losses. No sound, payments or additional game modes. Responsive mobile web; physical iPhone validation remains user-owned.
## Brand Commitments
User delegated a full redesign informed by Bruno Simon, Neal.fun, Linear, Lando Norris and Choo Choo World. Preserve all gameplay. User explicitly delegated visual selection.
